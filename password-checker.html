    How the score is calculated

    Strength comes from how many guesses an attacker would need. We estimate that from three things.

    Length

    Every extra character multiplies the work. Twelve characters or more is a solid minimum.

    Variety

    Lowercase and uppercase letters, numbers and symbols widen the pool each character is drawn from.

    Predictability

    Common passwords, repeats and keyboard runs like “qwerty” are guessed first, so they lower the score.

    Frequently asked questions

    Short answers to common questions.

    Is it safe to type my real password here?

    The check runs entirely in your browser and the page sends nothing over the network. Even so, the safest habit is to test a similar password rather than one you use today.

    What does “time to crack” mean?

    It’s an estimate assuming an offline attacker making ten billion guesses per second. Real speeds vary with how the service stores passwords.

    Can this tell me if my password was leaked?

    No. It scores structure only and does not check breach databases. Use a breach-notification service to see whether an account was exposed.

    What makes a good passphrase?

    Four or more unrelated words are long and easy to remember. A password manager can generate and store unique passwords for every account.
